About Me

Hey!
Glad to see you here!
I’m Parisa, a web and social media analyst, fascinated by the spaces where epistemology, sociology, and academic analysis intersect.
My curiosity, the question of how I might weave myself into the flows of the world, first began halfway through my architecture bachelor in Iran. Through computational work in installations and robotics, my exploration gradually bridged into computational social sciences and media cultures. At the moment, I’m a master’s student in Media Architecture in Germany, Learning new tools and techniques daily, to be in dialogue with the worlds and to stay keen.
Contact me if you want to collaborate, share ideas, or just say hi!
