What is Poem Iris?

I was thinking about sending a poem to a friend, but indirectly, somehow not through language, but through the way his iris looked. That's how I developed Poem Iris.

Poem Iris translates words and sentences into a radial coordinate system. Instead of reading the poem as ordinary text, each sentence becomes a circular layer around the center.

Each sentence becomes a circular layer. The words are distributed around the circle, and each word becomes a small space of its own. Within that space, the letters are positioned according to two things: their order in the word and their position in the alphabet. The first letter begins closer to the center, the next one moves outward, and so on. At the same time, each letter finds its position around the word according to its alphabetic value, from A to Z. The letters are then connected in the order in which they appear in the word. This creates a visual trace of the language without displaying the language itself.

In this way, the structure of the language becomes a visual pattern. The poem is still there, but its form can be experienced without reading it directly.
