What is My Machinic Station?

My Machinic Station is a space where ideas are metaphorized through machinic assemblages. It's a term I borrow from Gilles Deleuze and Félix Guattari.

An assemblage is a temporary collection of things, people, tools, and relations that come together for a reason. Through these assemblages, desires can take form and express themselves.

I want to connect to people through my weirdest, rawest, unfinished ideas.

An idea does not exist by itself. It works through an assemblage: me, my keyboard, AI, my website, my philosophical books, the people I encounter.

If one part disappears, another has to take its place so that the desire can continue to express itself.

This is a prototype for experimenting with that process.
